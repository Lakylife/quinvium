Why Quinvium

A network secured by miners and usable by anyone who knows Ethereum.

Full EVM, familiar tools

Solidity, ethers.js, Hardhat and MetaMask work unchanged. Point them at the public RPC and deploy.

const provider = new ethers.JsonRpcProvider(
  'https://rpc.quinvium.com', 52401);
await provider.getBlockNumber();

Mined on graphics cards

Ethash on an ordinary AMD or NVIDIA card. Blocks are backed by work done, not by the size of a stake.

Fair launch

No premine, no presale, no company and no owner.

Fees to miners

Fees are not burned. The block miner keeps all of them.

Common questions

How do I get QU?
By mining, or by a transfer from someone who has QU. There is no presale and no exchange.
Did anyone get coins before launch?
No. The genesis has no balances; every QU is mined, by anyone.
Does MetaMask work?
Yes, as with any EVM network. Add the network with the button above.
Has there been an audit?
Internal reviews yes, an independent audit not yet. Quinvium is experimental software.
