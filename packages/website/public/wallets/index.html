Wallet safety

  • Write the recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline. Whoever knows it owns your funds.
  • Never share the phrase. Anyone who asks for it is a scammer.
  • Before signing, check the network (chain ID 52401), the address and the amount.
  • Verify tokens by contract address, not by name. Anyone can pick any name.
