Mining Quinvium

A graphics card, the app and a payout address. That is all.

What you need

A graphics card

AMD or NVIDIA with at least 2 GB of memory. The algorithm data (DAG) grows over time, so more memory lasts longer. Ethash ASICs are allowed too.

A payout address

Any 0x… address from your wallet. Rewards are paid straight to it.

A connection

The node joins the network on its own through seed.quinvium.com.

Mining in the Quinvium app

  1. Install the app for macOS or Windows. Download
  2. Choose the Mainnet network. The app starts its own node and downloads the chain.
  3. In Mining, enter your payout address and switch mining on.

Your own node and miner

The command-line node and the quintium-miner GPU miner:

quintium-node --network mainnet --miner-address VAŠE_ADRESA
quintium-miner --address VAŠE_ADRESA --node http://127.0.0.1:8545

The node also offers Stratum (port 3333), so other Ethash miners work too. For miners on your local network start the node with --stratum-host 0.0.0.0.

Solo or pool

Solo

You get the whole block and its fees, but with one card there can be a long wait between blocks.

Contract pool

The pool factory is built into the network. A pool counts miner shares and the contract pays rewards by them. Nobody runs a public mainnet pool yet.

Difficulty

Difficulty adjusts after every block based on the last 60 blocks (LWMA). When hashrate joins, blocks speed up for a while and difficulty catches up within minutes. When hashrate leaves, it settles the same way. The minimum difficulty is 224.

Mining questions

How much will I mine?
Your share of the network hashrate times 4 QU per block. About 5,760 blocks are made a day, roughly 23,040 QU for the whole network. The current hashrate is on the home page and in the Explorer.
How fast does the reward arrive?
Immediately with the block. The reward is part of the block you mine.
Can I mine on a cloud server?
That depends on the provider's terms. Many of them forbid mining.
