About Quinvium

A programmable proof-of-work network secured by miners and usable by anyone who knows Ethereum.

What Quinvium is

Quinvium (ticker QU) is an independent blockchain with its own node, wallets and Explorer. Miners produce blocks with the Ethash algorithm on graphics cards, and transactions and contracts run on the EVM, the same machine as in Ethereum.

The name comes from the Latin quinta essentia, the fifth essence. That is why the logo is a pentagon.

Why proof-of-work and the EVM

Proof-of-Work

The order of transactions is decided by work done, not by the size of a stake. Anyone with a graphics card can take part.

EVM

Contracts, tools and wallets from Ethereum work without changes. Developers have nothing new to learn.

Principles

  • No company or foundation runs the network and nobody owns it.
  • No premine, presale or developer allocation.
  • Built-in contracts have no owner.
  • Consensus rules change only by a hard fork that nodes must accept.
  • Honest about risks: the network is young and no independent audit has been done yet.
