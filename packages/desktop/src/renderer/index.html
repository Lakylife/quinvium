Pools

        
In a pool you get paid for your share of the work, so income stays steady even with a small card. A contract holds the rewards, so the operator cannot take them.

About

        

          

          
Quinvium

        

        
Quinvium is an independent proof-of-work blockchain compatible with Ethereum smart contracts. Anyone can run a node and mine with a graphics card. There is no premine, and all fees go to the miners.

        

          
          
Quinvium QU
Its coin, Quinvium (QU), can only be mined. No coin was created in advance, every one comes from real work.
